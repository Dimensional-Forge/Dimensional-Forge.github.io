Services

Senior product development, on demand

Ten services from one engineer. I run only the Forge Method phases your project needs, and I tell you up front what it will cost.

// SYS_ARCH

Systems Architecture

I define the whole product before anyone builds a piece of it: requirements decomposition, interfaces between subsystems, and system-level design reviews from concept to production.

// MECH_DESIGN

Mechanical & Electromechanical Design

SolidWorks CAD, GD&T and tolerance analysis for parts that have to fit, move and be manufactured, including the electromechanical assemblies where they meet the electronics.

// PROTO_DFM

Rapid Prototyping & DFM

Fast iterations from idea to physical part, with manufacturability designed in from the first print instead of patched in at the end.

// DOCS

PRD / FRD / SRD Writing

Product, functional and system requirement documents your team can build from and test against.

// RISK_ANALYSIS

DFMEA / SFMEA

I look for the ways your product can fail, at the design and system level, and design them out before a customer finds them.

// V_AND_V

Verification & Validation

Test plans, protocols and results that show, with evidence, that your product does what its requirements say.

// ROADMAP

Technical Roadmapping

A phased plan from where you are today to a shippable product, with milestones and budgets sized to your real constraints.

// COST_MODEL

Budgeting & Cost Modeling

Bottom-up cost and BOM models with margin checks, so you know whether the product makes business sense before you pay for tooling.

// DATA_SCI

Data Science & Analytics

Python and MATLAB analysis that turns test and product data into engineering decisions.

// LEAD_MENTOR

Leadership & Mentorship

I lead cross-functional teams of teams, facilitate design reviews, and mentor engineers and students through hands-on technical coaching.

About Sean Neal

I’m Sean Neal, a Senior Systems Engineer and product development consultant in Indianapolis. I analyzed rocket-engine test data at NASA Marshall Space Flight Center, interned twice at Tesla, did medical device R&D at SonaCare, and have spent eight years delivering connected hardware at Allegion, where I carried a WiFi smart-lock family from concept to retail shelves.

I started The Dimensional Forge so that experience could work for inventors, startups and small businesses who need senior engineering without a big firm’s overhead: from first concept through architecture, prototyping, validation and manufacturing readiness. When something goes wrong on a project I own it, say so plainly, and fix it.

Outside my day job I mentor FIRST Robotics Team 6956, co-lead the Minority Engineering Program of Indianapolis, and founded Allegion’s Pride Alliance; that work lives in the People & Project Leadership section. My leadership philosophy is simple: people first, systems second, ego last.
